# The rabbit hole

A card in your hand, a sky to read, and somewhere to put the kettle: Cartomancy grows from a travelling practice into a household. The same sky that strengthens a spell sets the workshop's hours and shines twice over the garden, once above and once on the pond.

Follow whichever part catches your eye. The sky is a useful beginning; the rest soon finds reasons to look up.
